How it works

Three roles. One simple flow.

FOR GUESTS

Book, then share

Book a private room directly. If you end up not needing the whole space, list it for sharing and split the cost with another traveller.

FOR HOTELIERS

List your property

Add your hotel and rooms from your own dashboard. Every listing is reviewed before it goes live, so guests always book with confidence.

FOR THE PLATFORM

Verified by SuperAdmin

A SuperAdmin approves hoteliers and properties, and keeps an eye on bookings and shared-room activity platform-wide.
